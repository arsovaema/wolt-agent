Prepare your Wolt cart

Version 0.1.5: If already installed, replace the old extracted files with the new download and click Reload on the helper’s extensions-page card. Refresh the planner. This update opens Wolt’s Your orders drawer, matches its meal-card buttons, and retains its preset radio options. New plans use one restaurant per day. Retry is available before any Add action.

Install the helper once in Chrome or Edge, in the browser profile where you use Wolt.

  1. Download Wolt Cart Helper and extract the ZIP.
  2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions. Turn on Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the extracted folder containing manifest.json.
  3. Sign in to Wolt in this browser and use its English interface. Reload the meal planner on localhost:8000 or 127.0.0.1:8000.
  4. Click a planned day, review the meals, then choose Yes, prepare in Wolt.

You review and pay on Wolt. Check your delivery address, time, quantities, fees and final total. Choosing a future plan day does not schedule delivery. Separate restaurants require separate checkouts.

The helper pauses for an existing cart, choices, changed prices or uncertain page controls. Follow its message and finish manually if needed. Use its Stop control on Wolt to stop preparation; closing the planner dialog does not cancel a sent request.

Experimental: v0.1.5 successfully added three meals in one live Healthy Junk Food cart (573 MKD); other restaurant layouts may still need adjustments. Payment is always yours. Your cookies and passwords stay in your browser. Remove the helper from the extensions page whenever you like.
